About Me

Hi! I’m a passionate Game Developer who not only loves making games,
but also playing them. While RPGs are my absolute favorite genre to
play, as a developer I enjoy the technical challenge of building all
kinds of genres, whether in 2D or 3D. When developing, my main focus
is on building gameplay mechanics, designing UI architectures, and
adding responsive interface effects that make interactions feel great.

Beyond development, I love attending video game conventions and jams
to connect with other professionals in the industry and discover new
amazing projects.

Take a look at my projects below to see what I’ve been working on!
